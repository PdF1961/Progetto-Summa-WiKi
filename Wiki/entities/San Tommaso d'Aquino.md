# San Tommaso d'Aquino (Thomas Aquinas, c. 1225–1274)

Dominican friar, theologian, and philosopher; central authority of Catholic philosophy and theology per Leo XIII's *Aeterni Patris* (1879) and CIC canon 1366/252. This page is the central hub entity for all Aquinas-related sources in the vault; expand as each primary/secondary source is ingested.

## Core doctrine (per [[essenza-e-attualita-del-tomismo]])

- Metaphysics of being (ens), not physics or psychology, is the root of his system.
- Division of being into **potency and act**, with the primacy of act — see [[Dottrina di Atto e Potenza]].
- Three characters that follow from this: realism, intellectualism, theocentrism — see [[Tomismo]].
- Real distinction between essence and existence in every creature; only in God are essence and *esse* identical.
- Analogy of being (*ens* is neither univocal nor equivocal across God and creatures, but analogical) — opposed to Scotus's univocity.

## Natural law and ethics (per [[god-philosophy-universities]] ch. 10)

Four types of law (eternal, natural, divine, human); primary precepts of natural law (no killing the innocent, no theft/deception/promise-breaking) are universal because they are exactly the preconditions for rational deliberation among any human beings whatsoever; secondary precepts (application) vary by circumstance. Cardinal virtues (temperance, courage, justice, prudence, following Plato) plus theological virtues (faith, hope, charity, infused not acquired, following Augustine) — see [[dopo-la-virtu]] and [[Alasdair MacIntyre]] for the parallel treatment in the Aristotelian virtue-ethics tradition MacIntyre later grounded in Aquinas.

## Soul and body (per [[god-philosophy-universities]] ch. 9)

The soul is the substantial form of the body (not a separable Platonic substance); personal identity as thinker is derivative from identity as a particular animal body, which is why a soul separated at death is "incomplete" and Aquinas holds resurrection of the body — not mere survival of an immaterial soul — is needed for full personal immortality.

## University education (per [[god-philosophy-universities]] ch. 11)

Prescribed order of study — logic → mathematics → natural science → moral/political philosophy → metaphysics/theology — mirrors the order in which human rational powers must actually develop toward the final end of perfected understanding, not administrative convenience.
