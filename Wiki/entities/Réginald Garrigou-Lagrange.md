# Réginald Garrigou-Lagrange (1877–1964)

French Dominican theologian, one of the 20th century's leading exponents of strict Thomism (often called "Garrigou-Lagrangian Thomism" or classical/Thomistic Revival Thomism). Taught for decades at the Angelicum (Pontifical University of St. Thomas Aquinas) in Rome, where his students included Karol Wojtyła (later John Paul II).

## Positions

- Sharp critic of Modernism and of Maurice Blondel's "philosophy of action," which he saw as redefining truth away from *adaequatio rei et intellectus* toward a pragmatist *adaequatio mentis et vitae* — see [[essenza-e-attualita-del-tomismo]].
- Defender of the strict, "essentialist" reading of St. Thomas against eclectic scholasticism (Suárez) and against Scotist univocity of being.
- Central to the drafting climate around the **XXIV Tesi Tomiste** (1914/1925) — see [[XXIV Tesi Tomiste]] — though the theses themselves were compiled by Guido Mattiussi.

## Known Works (in this vault)

- [[essenza-e-attualita-del-tomismo]] (1947 Italian ed. 2012) — actuality of Thomism, doctrine of act/potency
- [[la-sintesi-tomistica]] (French 1950 as *La synthèse thomiste*, Italian tr. 1953/2015) — full systematic synthesis of Thomistic philosophy and theology, 8 parts from metaphysics through moral theology
