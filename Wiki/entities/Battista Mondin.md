# Battista Mondin

Italian theologian and philosopher, author of the [[mondin-dizionario-tommaso]] (*Dizionario Enciclopedico del Pensiero di S. Tommaso d'Aquino*, Edizioni Studio Domenicano).

## Interpretive stance

Distinguishes himself from Gilson, Masnovo, Maritain, and Fabro on the "keystone" of Thomistic metaphysics: where they treat the real distinction between essence and the act of existing (esse) as the central doctrine, Mondin argues the true keystone is rather **esse conceived intensively** (as *actualitas omnium actuum*) — see [[Dottrina di Atto e Potenza]] for the detailed argument. Also stakes out positions on disputed questions among Thomists themselves, e.g. judging both Egidio Romano's (over-reified) and Suárez's (under-reified) readings of the essence/esse distinction as mistaken.
