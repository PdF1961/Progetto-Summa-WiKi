## A parallel Latin-Italian edition

Not previously noted in [[somma-teologica-struttura]] or the Batch 1 entry: each question in this PDF opens with the **original Latin prooemium**, e.g. q.27's *"Iª q. 27 pr. Consideratis autem his quae ad divinae essentiae unitatem pertinent, restat considerare de his quae pertinent ad Trinitatem personarum in divinis..."*, immediately followed by the Italian `ARGOMENTO` translation of the same passage. This means the Latin text is directly checkable in this same file for any passage of interest, without sourcing a separate critical edition — worth remembering for future close-reading sessions on this source, especially where translation nuance matters (e.g. technical terms like *hypostasis*, *suppositum*, *relatio subsistens*).

## Structure, confirmed from each question's own prologue

Aquinas states the treatise's internal order explicitly at q.27's opening: since the divine Persons are distinguished by relations of origin, the material proceeds **processions (q.27) → relations (q.28) → persons (q.29)**. Within "persons," the order is: persons considered absolutely in general (meaning of the term, q.29; number, q.30; modes of expressing unity/plurality, q.31; our knowledge of them, q.32), then absolutely and individually (Father, q.33; Word, q.34; Image, q.35; Holy Spirit, q.36; Love, q.37; Gift, q.38), then comparatively — persons related to essence (q.39), to relations/properties (q.40), to notional acts (q.41), to each other in equality/likeness (q.42), and finally to creatures via mission (q.43). This is a clean, explicitly nested outline in Aquinas's own words, confirming [[La Santissima Trinità (Sintesi Tomistica)]]'s account (built from Garrigou-Lagrange) article-for-article.

## Processions (q.27 a.1)

The opening article's first objection states the core difficulty sharply: *"Processione significa movimento... Ma in Dio non vi è nulla che sia mobile"* — how can there be procession (which implies motion/exteriority) in a being that is pure act and absolute simplicity? Aquinas's *emanatio intelligibilis* solution (an interior, non-spatial procession, illustrated by the mind's word proceeding from the intellect) is the primary-text ground for [[La Santissima Trinità (Sintesi Tomistica)]]'s "two processions" section — the article itself not fully re-transcribed here, but its opening tension is now directly verified rather than only known via synthesis.

## The definition of person (q.29 a.1)

Confirms directly: *"la definizione della persona data da Boezio [De duab. nat. 3], cioè «la persona è una sostanza individuale di natura razionale»"* (Latin: *rationalis naturae individua substantia*) — the exact formula [[Persona (Tomismo)]] and [[La Santissima Trinità (Sintesi Tomistica)]] build on. The article opens by defending Boethius's definition against objections (e.g. that singulars aren't properly "defined") before Aquinas endorses and refines it for the divine case in aa.3-4 (whether "person" applies to God at all, and what it signifies there — answered via the *relatio subsistens* doctrine).

## Relations constitute the hypostases (q.40 aa.3-4)

Read closely, this is the primary-text location of the "circularity objection" [[La Santissima Trinità (Sintesi Tomistica)]] flags as "the hardest objection ever raised" against the Augustinian account. Aquinas's resolution, stated plainly at a.4: relations *"implicano esse stesse il proprio soggetto [o ipostasi], in quanto sono tutt'uno con le persone sussistenti: come la paternità è lo stesso Padre"* — relation and hypostasis are not really two things where one could ground the other in a vicious circle; they are the same reality considered under different aspects, so "removing" the relation mentally also removes the hypostasis, not just the "personality" layered onto a prior, relation-free hypostasis (the view of unnamed opponents refuted in the article). Aquinas cites Boethius's definition again here to make the point: since every hypostasis of rational nature just *is* a person, no hypostasis-without-personhood is coherently available as a fallback position for those who would separate relation from subject.

## What remains unread

qq.28, 30-35, 36-39, and 41-43 are confirmed structurally (each question's own topic-list prologue, extracted and quoted or paraphrased above) but not read article-by-article — in particular q.36's four articles on the Holy Spirit's procession (*filioque*-adjacent material) and q.43's treatment of the divine missions (the Trinity's indwelling in the just soul, the subject of [[La Santissima Trinità (Sintesi Tomistica)]]'s closing section on rival Vázquez/Suárez/Aquinas accounts) are natural next targets given the page map above.
