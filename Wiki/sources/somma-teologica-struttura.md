# Source: Somma Teologica — structural entry

**Author**: San Tommaso d'Aquino (unfinished; Supplementum by Reginald of Piperno)
**This edition**: Italian translation by P. Tito S. Centi O.P. (Convento di S. Domenico, Fiesole) — originally published Florence, 1949-1975; this online edition (2009) revised by P. Angelo Zelio Belloni O.P., "radicalmente rivista e modificata soprattutto nelle introduzioni, nella struttura e nei contenuti."
**Format**: single PDF, **5,318 pages**, 28.6MB

## Why this is a structural entry, not a full ingest

At 5,318 pages, this file *is* essentially the entire *Summa Theologiae* — there is no meaningful sense in which it can be "read completely" in one ingest pass the way [[opuscoli-tommaso]] or even [[sul-male-de-malo]] could be substantially sampled. As agreed with the user for this batch's oversized sources, this entry instead **verifies and records the work's structure directly from its own front-matter index** (extracted from PDF pages 1-40, which contain a compact master table of contents for all four parts before the actual question-by-question text begins around page 40), so that future sessions can navigate directly to a specific treatise/question rather than re-deriving structure from general knowledge.

## Verified structure (extracted directly from the PDF's own index)

- **Prima Pars (I)** — Prologo; qq. 1-36 confirmed directly (q.1 *La dottrina sacra*; q.2 *Trattato su Dio: l'esistenza di Dio*; q.3-11 divine attributes — simplicity, perfection, goodness, infinity, presence, immutability, eternity, unity; q.12-13 our knowledge and naming of God; q.14-26 God's knowledge/ideas/truth/will/love/justice-mercy/providence/predestination/power/beatitude; q.27-43 the Trinity — processions, relations, persons, the Son as Image, the Holy Spirit); continues (not re-verified line-by-line here) through the treatises on **creation, the six days, angels, man (body/soul/faculties), the government of creatures** to q.119 (standard count).
- **Prima Secundae (I-II)** — confirmed directly qq. 99-114 (the Old Law's precepts, moral/ceremonial/judicial precepts and their causes/duration, the New/Evangelical Law compared to the Old, the necessity and essence of grace, grace's division/cause/effects — justification of the sinner, **q.114 "Il merito"**). Standard total: 114 questions, covering (earlier, not re-verified here) the last end/beatitude, human acts, passions, habits, virtues and vices in general, law in general, and grace.
- **Secunda Secundae (II-II)** — confirmed directly qq. 1-43 (faith's object/interior-exterior act/virtue/subjects/cause/effects; the gifts of understanding and knowledge; unbelief, heresy, apostasy, blasphemy against the Holy Spirit; hope, fear, despair, presumption; charity in itself/subject/object/order, joy, peace, mercy, beneficence, almsgiving, fraternal correction, hatred, **sloth (accidia), envy**, discord, contention, schism, war, brawling, sedition, scandal) and the final question confirmed directly: **q.189 "L'entrata in religione."** Standard total: 189 questions — the largest part, covering the cardinal and theological virtues and their opposed vices, and the states of life (including q.171-178 on prophecy/rapture and q.179-189 on the active/contemplative life and religious states).
- **Tertia Pars (III)** — confirmed directly and **complete**, qq. 1-90: the fittingness and mode of the Incarnation (qq.1-6), Christ's grace/knowledge/power (qq.7-13), the defects/limits Christ assumed (qq.14-15), unity of Christ's being/will/operation (qq.16-19), Christ's subjection to the Father, prayer, priesthood, predestination, worship, mediatorship (qq.20-26), Mariology (qq.27-30), the conception, nativity, manifestation, circumcision, baptism, temptation, teaching, and miracles of Christ (qq.31-44), the Transfiguration, Passion, death, burial, descent into hell, Resurrection, Ascension, session at the Father's right hand, judicial power (qq.45-59), the sacraments in general (qq.60-65), Baptism, Confirmation, the Eucharist (transubstantiation, real presence, accidents without substance) (qq.66-83), and Penance (qq.84-90, **ending mid-treatise at "Le parti della penitenza in generale"** — the point at which Aquinas's own writing breaks off, consistent with [[le-thomisme-gilson]]'s bibliography noting the *Tertia Pars* as "*Inachevée*").
- **Supplementum** — confirmed directly to begin at q.1 "La contrizione," continuing the treatise on Penance where Aquinas stopped, then covering Extreme Unction, Holy Orders, Matrimony, and the Last Things (death, judgment, resurrection, heaven, hell) — compiled posthumously by **Reginald of Piperno** (Aquinas's secretary/companion) from Aquinas's earlier *Scriptum super Sententiis*, not from the *Summa*'s own mature voice. Standard count: ~99 questions.

## Significance for this vault

This is the primary reference point behind nearly every secondary source already ingested — cited constantly by [[la-sintesi-tomistica]], [[god-philosophy-universities]], [[mondin-dizionario-tommaso]], and [[le-thomisme-gilson]]. Having its verified structure on file lets future sessions jump directly to, e.g., *S.Th.* I, q.2-3 for the five ways, I, q.44-46 for creation, I-II qq.90-108 for law, or III qq.60-83 for the sacraments, rather than needing to re-derive the map each time.

## Flagged for follow-up deep ingest

Any future deep-ingest of this source should proceed **treatise by treatise**, not attempt the whole work at once — natural units already flagged elsewhere in this vault as high-value: *De Deo Uno* (I, qq.2-26, cross-references [[la-sintesi-tomistica]] Parte Seconda), the Trinity (I, qq.27-43, cross-references [[la-sintesi-tomistica]] Parte Terza), the treatise on law (I-II, qq.90-108, cross-references [[god-philosophy-universities]] ch.10), and the Christological/sacramental material in Tertia Pars (cross-references [[la-sintesi-tomistica]] Parti Quinta-Sesta).
