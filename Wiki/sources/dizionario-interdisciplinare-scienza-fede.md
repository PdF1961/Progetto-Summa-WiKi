## What this work is (general knowledge, unverified against this file)

A major two-volume Italian encyclopedic dictionary (first published 2002, Urbaniana University Press in collaboration with Città Nuova) surveying the interdisciplinary dialogue between the natural sciences and the Catholic theological/philosophical tradition. Entries, contributed by specialists across physics, biology, cosmology, neuroscience, epistemology, philosophy of science, and theology, typically cover topics such as: the epistemological status of scientific vs. theological knowledge, the relationship between evolutionary biology and creation doctrine, cosmology and the origin of the universe, the mind-body problem and neuroscience, miracles and natural law, the history of the Galileo affair and church-science relations, and biographical entries on key scientist-theologians.

## Relevance to this vault

Only tangential to the vault's current Thomistic focus — this is a broader "faith and science" reference work, not a Thomistic text specifically, though it likely contains entries directly relevant to Thomistic natural philosophy (e.g., entries on causality, natural law, the soul, teleology) given Aquinas's centrality to Catholic intellectual tradition. Its size and scope make it better suited as a **reference work to consult for specific topics** (once readable) than a candidate for any kind of general ingest.
